# Lessons

Generalizable lessons harvested from every repo's `**General:**` lines and promoted here by curation PR only. At most 15 may be unmechanized at once; a lesson that can be code becomes a change to the skill or template that produces it, and its Status says so.

Format, one section per lesson:

```markdown
## <Rule, as an imperative>

- Why: <what happened, scrubbed of repo and session names>
- Mechanism: <script | hook | template | lint rule | none>
- Status: mechanized in <skill>@<sha> | mechanize by <trigger>
- Stacks: <node | ios | python | any>
```
